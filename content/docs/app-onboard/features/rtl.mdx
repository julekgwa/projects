# RTL Support

The slider supports right-to-left layouts for languages like Arabic and Hebrew.

<a href="https://snack.expo.dev/@lekgwaraj/react-native-app-onboard-rtl?platform=ios">

</a>

## Follows the device direction

By default the slider follows the device writing direction
(`I18nManager.isRTL`), so apps localized for RTL languages get a right-to-left
onboarding flow automatically — the footer buttons and pagination use
direction-aware layout.

## Forcing a direction

Force a direction independent of the device with the `rtl` prop:

```jsx
<Onboarding pages={pages} rtl />
```

When the requested direction differs from the device direction, the slider
(pages, dots, and progress bar) is mirrored so swiping and the indicators stay
consistent.

## Custom children

Forced mirroring applies to the declarative `pages` API. When you provide custom
children, RTL follows the device direction — lay out your own screens with
logical `start`/`end` styles (rather than `left`/`right`) so they adapt
automatically.
